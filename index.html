Frequently Asked Questions

Can I truly wear AJUBA jewelry in the shower, pool, and gym? +
Yes, absolutely! Our pieces are crafted using medical-grade 316L surgical stainless steel and coated with 18K Real Gold using Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD). This process bonds gold at a molecular level, preventing tarnishing from water, shampoo, perfume, and sweat.
What is the 1-Year Polish & Color Warranty? +
Every AJUBA purchase comes with a 1-year guarantee on color retention. In the rare event that your jewelry tarnishes or fades under normal everyday wear, we will replace or re-polish it free of charge.
What are the specifications of the new Watch collection? +
Our timepieces feature precision Japanese quartz movements, 3ATM water resistance (rain and splash proof), 316L anti-tarnish stainless steel cases, and scratch-resistant hardened mineral glass.
What is the shipping time and is Cash on Delivery (COD) available? +
We deliver all orders across India within 3–5 business days via premium express couriers (Bluedart & Delhivery). Cash on Delivery is available across 19,000+ pin codes. Orders over ₹2,000 qualify for free shipping.
